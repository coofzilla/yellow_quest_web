01

On your device

YellowQuest stores your profile, preferences, quests, discoveries, badges, rewards and journey summaries to run the app. Saved GPS samples are retained for active or recoverable journeys and removed when a journey is saved as completed or discarded. The current route may remain in memory for an optional overlay export until you clear it, start another journey or close the app. Recorder diagnostics contain counts and error codes, not precise coordinates.

02

Private iCloud sync

When sync is enabled, Apple CloudKit stores your private save so devices using the same iCloud account can share progress. This includes your profile and optional photo, rewards, quests, adventures, home and quest destinations, discovered map cells and associated times. Raw GPS recordings are excluded. Private saved locations are still location data, even though raw routes are not uploaded.

03

Premium and YellowPoints

Apple processes monthly and yearly Premium subscriptions. YellowQuest uses verified StoreKit entitlements to unlock Premium and receives no payment-card details. There is no separate YellowQuest account or account sign-in in this version. Subscriptions follow the device's App Store account and are managed through Apple.

YellowPoints are earned through gameplay, stored on your device and synchronized in your private iCloud save. They cannot be bought in this version. Gameplay and location observations are not sent to a separate YellowQuest account or points server.

Deleting the app or its data does not cancel your Apple subscription. Manage or cancel it through Apple’s subscription settings.

04

Maps and location

With your permission, location supports discovery, journeys and quest check-ins, including background check-ins when allowed by your settings. Apple MapKit handles maps, area searches and place lookups. Search terms, the searched area and requested places are sent to Apple. Opening navigation sends the chosen destination to your selected map provider. You can change location access in device Settings.

05

Public rankings and parties

Publishing a public profile shares your chosen name and optional photo, ranking stats and a stable public identifier. Joining a party publishes the party name and photo, member ranking stats, membership identifiers and join dates used to verify group scores. Rankings do not publish your points balance, home, quest destinations or live location.

When you share an adventure, its quest destinations become available to your party through CloudKit sharing. Party members can see membership names and shared quest check-in progress. Optional location sharing is off by default. When you turn it on for a party, your latest location, accuracy, update time and photo from Profile are shared with all current and future members through the private CloudKit share, including while the app is in the background with Always location access. YellowQuest keeps one latest location record per member, not a route history. Locations older than two minutes disappear from the app’s map; this display expiry does not itself erase the CloudKit record. Sharing controls apply to each device. If multiple devices share, the newest fix is displayed. Stopping sharing clears the coordinate and photo when the latest fix came from this device; another device may continue sharing until you stop it there too. Leaving, removal or ending the party deletes the location record when the cloud operation succeeds. A failed stop stops this device’s GPS sharing immediately and shows a connection message. Invitation links give access to an invitation; send them only to people you intend to invite.

06

Reports and blocking

Public names are filtered, and Apple’s on-device Sensitive Content Analysis checks public photos when enabled in iOS Settings. Screening results are not uploaded. Reports send your selected reason, optional details, the reported name and photo, and account identifiers to CloudKit for moderator review. Reports are not public listings. Your blocked-account list is stored privately for your iCloud account. Blocking hides that person’s public content, invitations and location marker; it does not leave or remove members from an existing party. Members of a CloudKit share retain access to its shared records. Stop location sharing or leave/remove the member to end that sharing relationship.

Deleting your YellowQuest data removes your reports and blocked list. Minimal moderation identifiers may remain to enforce restrictions and prevent abuse. Reports remain until deleted by their author through account deletion or removed by a moderator.

07

Your controls

You can remove your public profile in Rankings and leave a group in Party. Sync and deletion controls are in Settings. Delete All Local Data clears this device only; cloud and public data remain. Delete YellowQuest Data, in iCloud settings, removes your cloud save, public profile, your party data and this device’s progress. It disbands parties you lead. An interrupted deletion stays pending for retry, with sync paused. Minimal deletion markers prevent old devices from uploading erased progress again. Start Fresh creates an empty save.

Other devices may retain offline copies until they reconnect and complete the deletion flow. Exports or copies held by other people cannot be recalled.

08

No advertising tracking

YellowQuest does not use advertising or cross-app tracking. Its data is used for app functionality. Photos are optional; the system photo picker gives access only to the images you select. Live Activities can display quest information on your Lock Screen when enabled.

09

This website

This website stores your selected theme and appearance in your browser’s local storage. It uses no analytics scripts, advertising cookies, or tracking pixels. You can clear these preferences in your browser settings.

When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security. Learn more in GitHub’s hosting documentation. Emailing support shares your email address and the information you choose to include so we can respond to your request.
